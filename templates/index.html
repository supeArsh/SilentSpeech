Model Architectures & Research Context

Recommended

Auto-AVSR (LRS3 Open-Vocabulary)

  • Dataset: 400+ hours of natural TED Talks (LRS3).
  • Vocabulary: 5,000 SentencePiece subwords (Open English).
  • Architecture: Conformer Encoder-Decoder with Visual Beam Search.
  • Best for: Real, unrestricted conversational English sentences.
Baseline

GRID Baseline (51 Words)

  • Dataset: GRID Audio-Visual Corpus (33 speakers, 26,000 clips).
  • Vocabulary: Strict 51-word closed vocabulary.
  • Architecture: 3D-CNN + ResNet-18 + Bidirectional GRU + CTC.
  • Grammar Formula: command + color + preposition + letter + digit + adverb.
"Lip reading without audio context is inherently ambiguous; even trained human lip readers achieve ~30% word accuracy on isolated silent video. SilentSpeech uses deep spatio-temporal representations to resolve viseme ambiguities."
